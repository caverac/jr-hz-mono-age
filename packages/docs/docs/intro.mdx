# 1. Introduction

This site will document the analysis behind the paper on mono-age disk populations and
the age dependence of the spiral $J_R$-$h_Z$ relation (Palicio et al. 2025). The
companion paper, [`jr-hz-relation`](https://github.com/caverac/jr-hz-relation), derives
the slope of the relation for a whole disk from the self-gravitating isothermal sheet.
This project tests how the slope behaves for populations of a single age, in seven
Auriga disks and in the Milky Way.

The pages follow the sections of the manuscript as they are written.
