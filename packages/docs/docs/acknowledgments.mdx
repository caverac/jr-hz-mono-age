# Acknowledgments

## The Auriga Project

The simulated disks of this project come from the Auriga Project, a suite of
cosmological magnetohydrodynamical zoom-in simulations of the formation of Milky
Way-mass galaxies (Grand et al. 2017), which its public data release extends to dwarf
galaxies (Grand et al. 2024). We use the $z = 0$ snapshots and group catalogs of seven
halos of the Original suite at Level 4: Au2, Au3, Au16, Au19, Au23, Au24 and Au27.

> We have used simulations from the Auriga Project public data release (Grand et al.
> 2024) available at
> [https://wwwmpa.mpa-garching.mpg.de/auriga/data.html](https://wwwmpa.mpa-garching.mpg.de/auriga/data.html)
